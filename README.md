# 📊 End-to-End E-Commerce Sales Analytics Project

## 🚀 Project Overview
This project focuses on performing **complete business sales analysis** using real-world global superstore data.  
The objective of this project is to derive **actionable business insights** related to revenue growth, customer behavior, product profitability, and regional sales performance.

The project demonstrates strong hands-on expertise in:

✔ Data Cleaning & Transformation  
✔ SQL Business Querying  
✔ Data Modeling  
✔ Advanced Power BI Dashboarding  
✔ Business KPI Analysis  

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Identify **top performing products and categories**
- Analyze **monthly sales and revenue growth trends**
- Understand **customer segmentation and repeat purchase behavior**
- Evaluate **regional sales contribution**
- Study **discount impact on profitability**
- Track **overall business KPIs for executive decision making**

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies Used

- **Excel** → Data Cleaning & Pre-processing  
- **SQL** → Business Analysis Queries  
- **Power BI** → Data Modeling & Interactive Dashboard  
- **DAX** → KPI Calculations & Advanced Measures

- Customer Count by Region
- Segment Contribution

### 🔹 Product Performance Insights
- Top 10 Products by Revenue
- Sub-Category Profitability Analysis
- Discount vs Profit Relationship
- Profit per Order KPI

---

## 📊 Key Business Insights Generated

✔ Technology category contributes highest revenue  
✔ High discount levels negatively impact profitability  
✔ Central region generates maximum sales  
✔ Certain sub-categories like Tables show consistent losses  
✔ Customer acquisition trend shows steady year-on-year growth  
✔ Few products contribute majority of revenue (Pareto effect)
